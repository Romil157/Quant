Statistical Rigor & Multiple Testing Methodology

  • Probabilistic Sharpe Ratio (PSR): Measures the probability that the strategy's true Sharpe ratio exceeds zero (SR* = 0), accounting for non-normal return distributions (skewness and excess kurtosis) and sample size n.
  • Deflated Sharpe Ratio (DSR): Extends PSR by replacing the zero benchmark with E[max SR₀] (the expected maximum Sharpe ratio under the null hypothesis of zero true skill across N=7 independent strategies). Corrects for selection bias, data mining, and multiple testing.
  • Circular Block Bootstrap (CBB): Resamples daily returns in overlapping blocks of L=20 trading days (B=500 iterations) to preserve autocorrelation and compute non-parametric 95% empirical confidence intervals.
  • Execution Assumptions: Commission=2.0 bps, Spread=1.0 bps, Slippage=2.0 bps, Long-only max position=100%, Max drawdown risk trigger=20%.
